The 4 elements

Warm, cool, moist, dry

The oldest personality test in the world sorts people into four temperaments by warmth and moisture. Hippocrates started it, Galen refined it, and Ibn Sina's Canon of Medicine taught it from Spain to India for six centuries. It still explains why one friend is always warm, hungry and in a hurry, and another needs a blanket and a quiet evening.

Your element shapes your energy, your moods and the foods that traditionally suit you. Combine it with your personality type and you get one of 64 Mizaj cards.
