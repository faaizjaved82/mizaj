Our story

From Ibn Sina to your phone

A thousand years ago in Bukhara and Isfahan, Ibn Sina wrote the Canon of Medicine. In it he described how every person carries a mizaj, a temperament of warmth and moisture that colours body and mind. For six centuries his book taught that idea from Spain to India, and we still use its words: a sanguine mood, a phlegmatic calm, a hot temper.

The four temperaments travelled further. Hippocrates and Galen described them first; Ibn Sina refined them; European thinkers turned them into theories of character; and twentieth-century psychologists built modern type theory, including the four-letter types millions now use, partly on that old foundation.

Mizaj brings the two together for everyone: modern personality psychology for how you think and decide, and the four temperaments for your energy, moods and the foods that traditionally suit you. It's for anyone who wants to understand themselves and other people: students choosing a path, professionals and managers, teachers, marketers, couples, parents and grandparents. Questions about everyday life and work, at any age. Careers with the route into each.
