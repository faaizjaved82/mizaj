Our story

From Ibn Sina to your phone

A thousand years ago in Bukhara and Isfahan, Ibn Sina wrote the Canon of Medicine. In it he described how every person carries a mizaj, a temperament of warmth and moisture that colours body and mind. Hakeems across our part of the world still read people this way, and so do our nanis when they say "iska mizaj garam hai."

The four temperaments travelled further. Hippocrates and Galen described them first; Ibn Sina refined them; European thinkers turned them into theories of character; and twentieth-century psychologists built modern type theory, including the four-letter types millions now use, partly on that old foundation.

Mizaj brings the two together for desis everywhere: modern personality psychology for how you think and decide, and Unani temperament for your energy, moods and the foods that suit you. Questions about weddings, cousins and chai, wherever you live. Urdu alongside English. Careers with the route into each.
