### 1.3 Urdu-language online career tests

- Eduvision's aptitude assessment is offered in Urdu. https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/aptitude-assessment-for-career-selection-news-4340

- **Finding:** the search surfaced no polished, mobile-first Urdu career test grounded in Pakistani streams. "Pakistani streams" here means Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS, I.Com and FA, plus MDCAT/ECAT paths.

### 1.4 Gaps mizaj.pk can fill

1. **Price gap.** Human counselling costs PKR 2,000 to 5,000 (up to 15,000) per session, and programmes cost PKR 6,000 to 12,000. A self-serve report at **PKR 500 to 1,500 [EST]** fits under that, with an optional upsell to a human counsellor (sources in 1.2).
2. **Language gap.** Few Urdu-first or Roman-Urdu, mobile-first tests exist (1.3).
3. **Decision-point fit.** The product should be built around Pakistani decisions:
   - which stream to choose after matric;
   - MDCAT vs ECAT vs BBA;
   - O/A Level subject choice;
   - how to answer the "doctor/engineer" family pressure.
4. **Parent report.** Families decide jointly, so a parent-facing Urdu summary is a likely differentiator **[EST, hypothesis]**.
5. **Timing.** The IBCC pass-mark change to 40% gives a new reason to talk about stream choice (1.1).

### 2.3 How to sell to schools (recommendations; [EST], not sourced facts)

- **Entry points:**
  - Chain head office: academic or student-services directorate.
  - Individual campus principals: franchise models (Educators, Allied) give campus heads budget latitude.
  - Cambridge "university counsellor" roles in A Level campuses.
- **Offer a free pilot to one grade at one campus**, then deliver:
  - a class-level dashboard for the principal;
  - a branded "Career Day" talk timed to class 9 subject selection and the A Level application season.
- **Let schools collect the fee from parents** as an activity fee. This avoids a procurement cycle.
- **Universities:** pitch freshman orientation (BNU, NUML-style counselling tests) and admissions outreach. Universities may pay for leads or co-branded "find your major" tests.

### 4.5 Cultural sensitivities in framing compatibility

- **Sect and caste matter.**
  - In a Karachi study, 42.2% wanted a spouse of the same sect, denomination or caste, and 52% said social norms influenced their choices. https://asianonlinejournals.com/index.php/AJSSMS/article/download/2442/1977/3723
  - Rural marriages are mostly within the caste, and inter-caste marriages face "intense social resistance", including violence. https://ojs.mul.edu.pk/index.php/JPRSS/article/download/683/543/3879
  - **Recommendation:** never score or filter on sect, caste or biradari.
- **Rishta apps already face scrutiny** for sect features and for whether they really give women autonomy. https://www.dawn.com/news/1957940 ; https://asianews.network/?p=243399
- **Framing recommendations [EST]:**
  - Present compatibility as "understanding each other: communication, conflict, expectations". Do not frame it as "will this marriage succeed".
  - Keep astrology out of the rishta product. Astrology is religiously contested, and astrologers work in "an increasingly repressive religious climate". https://restofworld.org/2024/youtube-astrology-stars-pakistan/
  - Offer a "family-safe" Urdu report that parents can read, alongside a private couple section.
  - Use halal-signalling language (Muzz uses "niyyat saaf").
  - Treat Valentine-style romance framing with care (see section 7).

## Where the money is for mizaj.pk (ranked; all prices and volumes are [EST])

### 1. School and college B2B career-assessment programmes (classes 9 to 12)

- **Why:** concentrated buyers with hundreds to 900+ branches (Beaconhouse, Educators, City School, Allied).
  - About 47% of secondary enrolment is private (§1.1).
  - Human counselling costs PKR 2,000 to 5,000 per session (§1.2).
  - Comparable Indian school programmes charge about ₹1,000 per student (§2.2).
- **Price:** PKR 500 to 1,000 per student for the online report and class dashboard; PKR 1,500 to 2,500 with a group workshop.
- **Volume:** 20 campuses × 250 students = 5,000 students per year. That is about **PKR 2.5M to 7.5M per year**.
- **Risk:** slow sales cycle. Start with franchise campus principals.

### 2. B2C Urdu and English student career report, with a counsellor upsell

- **Why:**
  - About 2.7M students reach the class 10 decision and about 1.5M the class 12 decision each year (§1.1).
  - No strong Urdu-first product exists (§1.3).
  - Demand spikes on result days (§7).
- **Price:** PKR 799 to 1,499 per report. Optional human session at PKR 3,000 to 5,000 through partner counsellors, with mizaj.pk taking a 20 to 30% share.
- **Volume:** a 0.1 to 0.3% conversion of the class 10 cohort gives about 2,700 to 8,000 reports. That is about **PKR 2M to 12M per year**, heavily concentrated in Jul to Sep.

### 3. Corporate team-personality workshops and team dashboards

- **Why:**
  - IBA charges PKR 40,000 to 90,000 per participant for open courses (§3.3).
  - MBTI-style content is already accepted in Pakistani L&D (§3.3).
  - Hiring assessment is crowded (The Talent Games, §3.1), so team-building is the open lane.
- **Price:** PKR 150,000 to 400,000 per in-house half-day for 20 to 30 people, including individual reports. SaaS team dashboard at PKR 1,000 to 2,000 per employee per year.
- **Volume:** 10 to 25 workshops per year. That is about **PKR 1.5M to 10M per year**, at high margin. Bank, telco and FMCG HR teams in Karachi and Lahore are the first targets.

### 4. Rishta and couple compatibility report (B2C, bureau white-label, diaspora tier)

- **Why:**
  - Over 6M users across DKR (4M) and Muzz (2M+) (§4.1).
  - Families already pay PKR 10,000 bureau registration and PKR 100,000 to 300,000 success fees (§4.2).
  - No mainstream psychometric couple report exists (§4.3).
- **Price:** PKR 999 to 1,999 per couple report in Pakistan; £/$/AED 9 to 19 for the diaspora. Bureau white-label at PKR 300 to 500 per report in bulk.
- **Volume:** 3,000 to 10,000 reports per year. That is about **PKR 3M to 15M per year** (wide range, unvalidated).
- **Risk:** cultural sensitivity (§4.5). Partnering with an app such as DKR could multiply volume.

### 5. Psych-test prep for ISSB, CSS and other selection boards (niche paid)

- **Why:** personality and psychological testing is compulsory in officer and civil-service selection, and academies charge about Rs 15,000/month (§3.1).
- **Price:** PKR 999 to 2,499 for a practice pack covering WAT/SCT-style exercises, personality self-insight and a mock report.
- **Volume:** unknown, because ISSB volumes were not found **[GAP]**. Budget conservatively at about PKR 0.5M to 3M per year.
- **Caution:** do not claim to "beat" official tests.

### Not ranked as revenue: fun modules (zodiac, numerology, AI palm)

- High attention (§5), but religiously sensitive and low willingness to pay.
- Use them as free, ad-light acquisition funnels into paid products #2 and #4.
- Ride the January prediction news cycle.

### Payments and positioning (recommendations, unverified)

- Accept JazzCash and Easypaisa for students and families, and cards or Stripe-type payments for the diaspora.
- Lead with the mizaj (Unani) angle as the culturally native hook, with the 16 types as the "modern" layer.
- Keep astrology visibly separate from the career and rishta products.
