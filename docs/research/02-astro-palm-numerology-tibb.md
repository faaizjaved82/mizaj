# Spiritual-tech and traditional-wisdom monetization: evidence for the mizaj.pk relaunch

Prepared 28 Oct 2026 for the mizaj.pk relaunch (expansion into astrology, palmistry, numerology and Unani/Tibb mizaj).

## Method and limits (read this first)

- **How facts were gathered.** WebFetch was blocked for every domain tried (inc42.com, entrackr.com, outlookbusiness.com, en.wikipedia.org, yespress.io all failed DNS resolution in this sandbox). Every fact below comes from **search-engine result summaries** (WebSearch, standard mode, plus one extended query). I did not open the underlying pages, so treat exact wording as "as reported in search snippets".
- **Search budget.** The session's shared WebSearch budget (200 calls per turn across all agents) ran out partway through section 4. As a result:
  - Sections 1 to 3 and part of 4 are web-sourced.
  - Sections 5, 6 (partly) and 7 rely on **background knowledge that I could not verify this session**. These items are marked **[UNVERIFIED]** and come with a list of what to check.
- **Labels used.**
  - **[EST]** marks third-party estimates.
  - **[CLAIM]** marks company self-reports.
  - **[MY CALC]** marks arithmetic I did myself.
- **No invented numbers.** Where sources conflict, both figures are shown.

## 1. Astrotalk (India) and South Asian peers

### 1.1 Astrotalk business model
- **What it is.** A pay-per-minute chat, call and video marketplace. Independent astrologers, tarot readers and numerologists set their own rates and availability. https://www.datingnews.com/apps-and-sites/astrotalk-astrologers-answer-relationship-questions/
- **Per-minute prices.** Calls range from ₹10 to ₹200 per minute, according to Inc42. https://inc42.com/?p=424056 A secondary blog says most astrologers on the live listing sit at ₹23–50 per minute **[EST]**, among https://roxyapi.com/blogs/build-astrotalk-competitor-astrology-app and https://miracuves.com/blog/what-is-astrotalk-how-it-works/
- **Revenue mix.** About 90% of revenue comes from one-on-one consultations. https://inc42.com/?p=424056
- **Take rate.** Not disclosed; Inc42 reports the company did not disclose its commission. https://inc42.com/?p=424056 Secondary blogs guess 15–25%, 20–25% or 30–40% **[EST, unreliable]**. https://miracuves.com/blog/business-model-of-astrotalk/ and https://www.thebusinessscroll.com/astrotalk-business-model/
- **Free first chat.**
  - The current chat page advertises a free first chat, then per-minute billing. https://miracuves.com/blog/what-is-astrotalk-how-it-works/
  - In 2022, first-time users got 5 free minutes. https://yourstory.com/2022/04/app-review-astrotech-startup-astrotalk-tarot-vedic-angel-reading/amp
  - One analysis calls the free first chat "the whole conversion mechanism". https://roxyapi.com/blogs/build-astrotalk-competitor-astrology-app
- **What people ask about.**
  - About 65% of revenue comes from marriage-related questions. https://inc42.com/features/can-astrotalk-chart-a-future-beyond-astrology/
  - US users focus more on relationships and finances; career questions are only 22% of US consultations. https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/marriages-in-india-breakups-in-new-york-astrotalk-astrology-data-shows-cultural-divide-92808.htm

### 2.3 Web2app funnels, conversion and refund benchmarks
- **Funnel stage benchmarks** (Adapty):
  - Quiz completion: 50–70%.
  - Quiz to paywall view: 70–85%.
  - Paywall to purchase: 2–5%.
  - Astrology is listed as a best fit for web2app ("tease hidden insights, unlock in app").
  - Source: https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/what-is-web-to-app-and-how-does-it-work.md
- **Other funnel figures.**
  - Web2app paywall conversion averages about 6%, about 3× in-app, per web2wave (a vendor). https://www.web2wave.com/post/best-web2app-funnel-builders-complete-guide-for-mobile-app-businesses
  - One app reported 3.8% install-to-paid in its app versus 6.1% visit-to-paid on its web funnel. https://community.web2wave.com/t/do-web-funnels-actually-convert-better-than-in-app-paywalls/1183
  - FunnelFox worked example: 10,000 clicks, about 1,300 reach the paywall, about 300 purchase. https://blog.funnelfox.com/web2app-funnels-101/
- **RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (115k+ apps, $16 B+ revenue):
  - Hard paywalls convert about 6× freemium, with similar retention (27% vs 28%).
  - 17% of new apps crossed $1k MRR in 2025, down from 19% in 2024.
  - Secondary coverage says 35% of annual-plan cancellations happen in month 1.
  - AI apps earn about 41% more per user but churn about 30% faster.
  - Sources: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps ; https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth ; https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/

### 4.2 Abjad / Ilm-e-Adad (Hisab al-Jummal)
- **Letter values.**
  - Each Arabic letter has a value: alif = 1, ba = 2 … up to 9, then tens and hundreds, ending with ghayn = 1000.
  - A word's or name's value is the sum of its letters. "Allah" = 66 (1+30+30+5).
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abjad_numerals
- **Variants and rules.**
  - The Maghribi (North African and Andalusian) order changes values for some letters at 60 and above; values below 100 are the same everywhere.
  - Each letter is counted once: shaddah is typically ignored, and alif maddah is not doubled.
  - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abjad_numerals ; https://bahaipedia.org/Abjad
- **Not the same as Western numerology.** Western name numerology uses A=1 … I=9 and reduces results to a single digit. Abjad is a separate system. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abjad_numerals
- **Calculators.** Existing tools such as https://abjadcalc.com/ show there is demand for name calculators.
- **How it is used in Pakistan** **[UNVERIFIED, search budget exhausted]**:
  - 786 as the abjad value of "Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim", widely written on letterheads, shops and vehicles in Pakistan.
  - Totalling the abjad values of a child's or bride and groom's names and reducing them (commonly modulo 9 or 12) to check "compatibility" or a "lucky" number, colour or day.
  - Choosing a business name whose total is considered favourable.
  - Taweez/amal practitioners using name totals.
  - Verify with Urdu-language sources such as Daily Jang and Roznama Dunya numerology columns, and YouTube "ilm-ul-adad" channels.

### 6.2 [UNVERIFIED] Islamic views (background knowledge; verify with Pakistani fatwa sources such as Darul Ifta Jamia Binoria, Dar al-Ifta Deoband, Minhaj and Javed Ghamidi's al-Mawrid)
- Mainstream Sunni scholarship treats predictive astrology (*ilm-un-nujoom* used to foretell events) and fortune-telling as impermissible. Commonly cited hadith:
  - Sahih Muslim 2230: visiting an *arraf* (fortune-teller) means prayers are not accepted for 40 nights. https://sunnah.com/muslim:2230
  - Abu Dawud 3905: learning astrology is "a branch of magic". https://sunnah.com/abudawud:3905

- Astronomy for time and qibla (*ilm-ul-hay'a*) is accepted.
- Palmistry, when used to predict, is treated like fortune-telling.
- Abjad as a *numbering and chronogram* system (tarikh-goi, 786) is widely tolerated. Using it to *predict* or for amulets is criticised by many scholars.
- Tibb/Unani mizaj is a medical and physiological tradition and is **not** religiously contested. It is culturally tied to Muslim scholars (Ibn Sina) and Tibb-e-Nabawi discourse. This makes it the lowest-risk vertical.

  - No claims about the future (ghaib).

## 7. The four-temperament lineage (brand story) **[UNVERIFIED: background knowledge; reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_temperaments (not fetched)]**

1. **Hippocrates** (c. 460–370 BCE): humoral theory, with health as a balance of four humours (blood, yellow bile, black bile, phlegm).
2. **Galen** (2nd century CE): turned humours into *temperaments* ("De Temperamentis"), as mixtures of the qualities hot/cold and wet/dry. This is the origin of the four Unani mizaj types:
   - garam-tar = sanguine (blood)
   - garam-khushk = choleric (yellow bile)
   - sard-tar = phlegmatic (phlegm)
   - sard-khushk = melancholic (black bile)
3. **Ibn Sina / Avicenna**: *al-Qanun fi'l-Tibb* (Canon of Medicine, early 11th century) systematised *mizaj* and its role in diet and treatment. It was the core text of Unani Tibb in South Asia (Delhi's Hakim Ajmal Khan tradition; Hamdard's founding ethos).
4. **Kant** (*Anthropology from a Pragmatic Point of View*, 1798): recast the four temperaments as psychological types of feeling and activity.
5. **Wundt, then Eysenck** (mid-20th century): mapped the temperaments onto two axes, extraversion and neuroticism (emotional stability):
   - sanguine = stable extravert
   - choleric = unstable extravert
   - phlegmatic = stable introvert
   - melancholic = unstable introvert

   This is the bridge from Tibb to modern psychometrics.
6. **Keirsey** (*Please Understand Me*, 1978): re-used the "four temperaments" idea for the 16-type world: Artisan (SP), Guardian (SJ), Idealist (NF), Rational (NT). His temperaments are *not* a one-to-one match with the Galenic four; avoid claiming they are. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keirsey_Temperament_Sorter (not fetched)

**Story line for mizaj.pk:** "From Hippocrates to Ibn Sina to Eysenck: the 2,400-year-old idea that your *mizaj* shapes how you feel, eat and relate." It is credible, Muslim-heritage-forward, and makes no predictive claims.
