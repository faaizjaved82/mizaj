# 05: What's working on social media for personality and self-discovery brands (research as of 28 Oct 2026)

Prepared for the Mizaj relaunch (international look, image style, carousel cards, result cards, content formats).

**How to read this file**
- Every fact has a source URL after it. "(snippet)" means the figure comes from a search-result snippet of that page,
  not from reading the page itself.
- **Limits:** WebFetch could not reach instagram.com, tiktok.com, socialblade.com or buffer.com from this session.
  Those hosts are outside the session's allowed network domains, so no live profile pages were opened.
  Follower counts come from search snippets of profile pages and from third-party trackers (CreatorDB, SPEAKRJ,
  vidIQ). Many are dated, so each one carries its date. A count marked "undated" can be years old. To verify, open
  the profiles on a phone, or add those domains to the session's network allow-list.
- "Estimate" or "inference" marks my own judgement. Nothing below is a made-up count. Where I found no count, I
  say so.
- Competitor and framework names (16Personalities, MBTI and so on) appear only because this is research. Per
  `content/STYLE.md`, none of them may appear in Mizaj product or social content.

## Executive summary (the 10 findings that matter most)

1. **The biggest personality product is not the biggest social account.** 16Personalities' official Instagram
   (@16personalitiesofficial) shows about 66K followers (snippet, undated)
   (https://www.instagram.com/16personalitiesofficial/), and its X account has 29.1K (https://x.com/16personalities).
   The brand spreads through its test, its result page and its 16 characters: fans redraw those characters on
   Pixiv and X (https://www.pixiv.net/en/tags/16personalities, https://x.com/cloumello/status/1768618111425794400).
   Co-Star does have a big account: about 2M on Instagram (snippet) (https://www.instagram.com/costarastrology/)
   and 411.3K on Threads (https://www.threads.com/@costarastrology).
2. **The share card is the growth engine.** Spotify Wrapped 2025 got 500M+ shares in its first 24 hours (up 41%)
   and 200M engaged users (https://musically.com/2025/12/05/spotify-wrapped-2025-attracted-over-200m-users-in-first-day/,
   https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/spotify-wrapped-2025-success-1235477520/). Its 2025 cards used a
   handmade "mixtape" look of doodles, stickers and cut-outs
   (https://www.prweek.com/article/1942739/no-one-wants-unwrap-thing-every-year-spotify-wrapped-shook-things-year).
   The two features that took off socially were a named tribe ("Clubs") and one provocative number ("Listening
   Age") (https://www.axios.com/2025/12/03/spotify-wrapped-2025-new-features).
3. **Group compatibility is the most shareable mechanic.** The viral "MBTI Chemistry" quiz scores a whole friend
   group of up to 10 people on a colour scale from blue (Perfect) to red (Disaster)
   (https://sportskeeda.com/pop-culture/what-tiktok-s-mbti-chemistry-quiz-where-find-viral-game-revealed,
   https://www.dexerto.com/tiktok/how-to-do-tiktoks-viral-mbti-chemistry-quiz-1933298/). Wrapped 2025 added a
   group "Wrapped Party" (https://www.techradar.com/news/live/spotify-wrapped-2025-live). In October 2026 a Korean
   saju compatibility site spread by inviting friends (https://koreabridge.net/post/ai-saju-websites-go-viral-korea).
4. **Hand-made and "imperfect" is the premium signal of 2026, and AI-looking imagery is a liability.** Canva's
   2026 report is titled "Imperfect by Design" and says DIY and collage elements are up 90%
   (https://cmotech.news/story/canva-backs-imperfect-by-design-trend-in-2026-report,
   https://www.creativebloq.com/design/canvas-2026-trend-predictions-have-filled-me-with-hope). Landor's global
   ECD calls the trend "Anti-AI Crafting"
   (https://designmagazine.com.au/anti-ai-crafting-the-50-million-handmade-rebellion-reshaping-design-in-2026/).
   The March 2025 AI "Ghibli" wave drew heavy backlash
   (https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/the-controversial-chatgpt-studio-ghibli-trend-explained-what-you-need-to-know).
5. **Collectibles are culturally hot.** Labubu's blind boxes, unboxing videos and scarcity created a global craze
   (https://www.cbc.ca/1.7569787, https://www.mentalfloss.com/fun/toys/psychology-behind-labubu-blind-box-craze).
   In YPulse data cited by Axios, trading cards are the number one item that males aged 13–39 collect
   (https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/08/26/pokemon-trading-cards-are-fueling-a-retail-boom-thanks-to-gen-z/).
   In April 2025, the AI "action figure / starter pack" trend put people in collectible packaging
   (https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/chatgpt-got-another-viral-moment-with-ai-action-figure-trend/).
   A result shown as a collectible card fits this moment.
6. **"Aura" is now everyday vocabulary.** People read personality from aura colours, use AR aura filters and post
   aura gradients (https://medium.com/global-voices-for-all/tiktoks-aura-trend-gen-z-s-new-astrology-61d3b957ac5a,
   https://www.accio.com/business/how_to_do_the_aura_trend_on_tiktok). "Aura farming" made Oxford's Word of the
   Year shortlist (https://techacute.com/aura-farming-oxfords-shortlisted-word-of-the-year-explained). The
   gradient look is everywhere, so it is crowded.
7. **Carousels win engagement; video wins reach.** On Instagram, Buffer's 2026 data (as reported) shows carousels
   at about 6.9% engagement by reach against about 3.3% for Reels, while Reels reach more people
   (https://blog.mean.ceo/instagram-carousel-engagement-statistics/,
   https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/). On TikTok, studies conflict:
   Fanpage Karma found photo carousels +81% on engagement
   (https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/), while Buffer found
   video ahead (3.39% vs 1.92%) (https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/).
8. **"Sends per reach" is the signal to design for.** Instagram's head reportedly named watch time, likes per
   reach and sends per reach as the top ranking signals in January 2025 (secondary reports)
   (https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers). A post that people send
   to a specific friend ("send this to your Fire friend") is the format to build.
9. **The four temperaments already have a TikTok niche, but no dominant brand.** TikTok's discover page for
   "choleric temperament" shows about 1M posts (https://www.tiktok.com/discover/choleric-temperament). Quiz videos,
   blended types such as "mel-chol", and tagging fictional characters are the recurring formats
   (https://www.tiktok.com/discover/temperament-quiz,
   https://www.tiktok.com/discover/phlegmatic-choleric-melancholic). I found no brand that owns the space
   (inference).
10. **Culture-rooted brands that go global modernise the form and keep the substance.** Sulwhasoo turns
    traditional Korean forms into contemporary luxury design and anchors the brand in ginseng research
    (https://www.glossy.co/beauty/how-sulwhasoo-is-blending-heritage-and-innovation-to-reach-us-consumers). Korean
    saju services relaunched in English frame readings as self-insight rather than fate
    (https://springfield-tv.com/press-release/2026-03-30/30997/saju-com-korea-top-ranked-fortune-service-with-5-million-users-launches-in-english,
    https://www.seoulz.com/korea-saju-industry/). The failures were cliché and symbol errors, as in luxury Lunar
    New Year campaigns (https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/how-lunar-new-year-became-overly-commercialized-what-it-means-n1257545).

### 1c. Large 16-type meme accounts, Enneagram and K-culture

- **16-type meme accounts (Instagram, Jan 2022 ranking):** @infpmemesdaily about 195K, @mbti_as_things about 131K,
  @enfpmemesdaily about 115K (https://mbtimemesdaily.medium.com/top-25-biggest-mbti-accounts-on-instagram-jan-2022-8da0903076d8).
  @mbti.memess showed about 92K in an undated snippet (https://www.instagram.com/mbti.memess/). I found no ranking
  for 2025–26.
- **Format history:** the 4×4 "16 types as…" grid began in Tumblr fandom around 2013, and one meme site counts
  53M+ TikTok posts under #mbtimemes (https://meme.com/memes/myers-briggs-type-indicator-mbti). Type-specific
  posts earn more engagement than general ones, which pushes creators to exaggerate the types
  (https://www.earlyyears.tv/?p=539923).
- **Enneagram:** @EnneagramAndMemes had 160K+ followers (older Daily Beast profile)
  (https://thedailybeast.com/whats-your-number-meet-the-enneagram-personality-type-influencers). Commentators say
  these accounts oversimplify the system (https://kansascitymag.com/tag/ashton-creates/).
- **Korean 16-type culture:**
  - Korean idols list their type in their profiles, to connect with fans (https://koreatravelpost.com/?p=12744,
    https://www.thestar.com.my/lifestyle/living/2025/10/03/why-k-pop-idols-share-their-personality-types-with-fans).
  - Fans build their own "fan type" tests (https://www.soompi.com/article/1391307wpp/whats-your-fan-mbti-type-fans-create-personalized-tests-for-k-pop-idol-fans).
  - Korean brands ran product-matching type tests such as "perfume type" and "snack type" (attribution from a
    search snippet of CNN coverage syndicated at https://ktvz.com/?p=918840).
  - In 2026, "Color" 16-type cards cross the 16 types with 4 personal-colour seasons to make 64 identity cards. The
    claim of 3× the share rate is the vendor's own data, so treat it as unverified
    (https://chatimg.ai/en/blog/color-mbti-explained).
  - In July 2025, "Egen-nam vs Teto-nam" sorted people by "energy or aura", and fans applied it to idols and
    fictional characters (https://blog.delivered.co.kr/k-lifestyle/korean-culture/new-personality-test-trend-replacing-mbti/).
- **Korean saju (Four Pillars) going global:**
  - Saju.com, which says it has 5M users, launched in English in March 2026 with content "redesigned for global
    users" (https://springfield-tv.com/press-release/2026-03-30/30997/saju-com-korea-top-ranked-fortune-service-with-5-million-users-launches-in-english).
  - Forteller reports about 628K monthly active users, up 28% year on year (https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026051116060499682).
  - "Saju" mentions in Korean Tinder profiles roughly quadrupled (same source).
  - 68% of Koreans in their 20s say they are interested in saju or tarot (Trend Monitor, same source).
  - Gen Z treats it as entertainment and "motivational content for self-comfort" (https://k-insider.com/en-us/articles/1780111231).

## 2. Which aesthetics are winning, rising or fading (late 2025 to 2026)

## 3. Which formats get the best reach right now

**Format performance (studies conflict, so read the denominators)**
- **Instagram:**
  - Carousels lead engagement in most 2026 datasets. Buffer's 2026 report, as summarised by others: about 6.9%
    engagement by reach for carousels against about 3.3% for Reels (https://blog.mean.ceo/instagram-carousel-engagement-statistics/,
    https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/).
  - Reels lead reach: a reported 30.81% reach rate, 1.36× that of carousels (same sources).
  - Measured against followers instead, the gap nearly disappears: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, single images
    0.37% (Socialinsider 2026, as summarised in https://oakgen.ai/blog/do-carousels-perform-better-what-2026-data-says).
- **TikTok:**
  - Fanpage Karma (about 700K posts, Jan–May 2025): carousels reached about 3% more accounts, engagement was about
    81% higher, but the share rate was about a third lower (https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/).
  - Buffer (2026) found the opposite: video 3.39% median engagement vs 1.92% for carousels (https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/).
  - Metricool (2.3M posts, early 2025 to early 2026; URL not pinned in results): images and carousels got about a
    fifth of video views, their engagement nearly halved year on year, and brands posted 140% more of them.
    TikTok photo mode is getting crowded (inference).
- **What to do:** use carousels for saves and engagement, short video for reach, and repurpose each carousel as a
  TikTok photo slideshow and a Reel.

**Ranking signals:** Instagram reportedly names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Treat the claim
that sends count 3–5× more than likes as unverified marketer folklore
(https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers,

**Image sizes**
- Instagram's profile grid moved to 3:4 in 2025. 1080×1440 is the common recommendation, with 1080×1350 (4:5) as
  the safe choice. Ads still use 4:5, and a carousel locks to its first slide's ratio

- Stories, TikTok and Reels share cards: 1080×1920 (9:16) (https://www.skills.sh/khadinakbarlabs/expo-mobile-app-builder/design-shareable-result-card).

**Practitioner guidance**
- A share card needs a "status signal": a big, satisfying number, rank, streak or score that makes the user look
  good (https://www.skills.sh/khadinakbarlabs/expo-mobile-app-builder/design-shareable-result-card).
- One quiz builder suggests making the result "a bright, downloadable card" that looks good in a feed, and putting
  the share button directly under the result rather than at the bottom (practitioner anecdote, via search
  snippet).
- Caution on rarity percentages: stacking traits makes almost everyone look "rare", and rarity figures come from
  self-selected test-takers (https://hmmm-2l9.pages.dev/en/learn/personality-rarity,
  https://jobcannon.io/research/distributions/mbti).

**Anatomy of a share-worthy result card (synthesis)**
1. A named identity in large type, with a two-part name (e.g. "The Poet · Water").
2. Owned art: a character or emblem people would want as a profile picture or sticker.
3. One status number: rarity from Mizaj's own aggregate data, labelled as such (e.g. "1 in 23 Mizaj test-takers").
4. Three short "so true" lines: specific, a little roasting.
5. A social hook: "Best with: Fire" and "Tag your opposite", plus a group mode.
6. Brand and URL in a corner that survives cropping, outside the platform's interface zones.
7. 9:16 for Stories and TikTok, plus a 4:5 or 3:4 version for the feed.

  - Wrong tone: Burberry's bleak, horror-like images (https://www.bfm.my/podcast/bigger-picture/the-daily-digest/burberrys-blunder).

**What this means for an Ibn Sina temperament brand aimed at a global audience (inference)**
1. **Lead with the universal layer, then reveal the lineage.** Mizaj's own STYLE v2 already puts the element
   first (Air, Fire, Water, Earth), the Unani name second and the Greek name as context. Keep that order in
   visuals too: element symbol and colour first, Unani name as a small second line.
2. **Tell the lineage as a story, not an aesthetic.** "A 2,400-year-old personality system: Hippocrates → Galen →

3. **Use one signature motif, used with restraint.** The girih eight-pointed star becomes a grid, a frame corner
   or a foil emblem, the way Sulwhasoo uses traditional forms. Avoid orientalist props (camels, lamps, domes,
   arabesque overload) and village scenes. STYLE v2 already says modern, urban and global.
4. **Get the symbols right and stay faith-inclusive.** No religious symbols as decoration. The art brief already
   forbids them.
5. **Commission one human artist for the master style.** The Margiela lesson: an artist's authorship is itself the
   cultural authenticity. Then extend that style consistently (section 6).

## 6. Image generation: what reads as premium vs "AI slop" in 2026

**Reads premium now**
- Visible human hand: ink lines, brush texture, risograph grain, paper scans, collage and cut-out edges
  (https://cmotech.news/story/canva-backs-imperfect-by-design-trend-in-2026-report,
  https://designmagazine.com.au/anti-ai-crafting-the-50-million-handmade-rebellion-reshaping-design-in-2026/).
- Commissioned illustration with authorship, which luxury brands now treat as an "anti-slop strategy"
  (https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/2026-is-the-year-of-the-fashion-illustrator/).
- Analog and zine layouts (https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/graphic-design-trends-2026).
- A narrow, owned palette and a limited set of symbols.
- Type as the image: expressive serifs and distortion
  (https://www.creativebloq.com/design/fonts-typography/breaking-rules-and-bringing-joy-top-typography-trends-for-2026).

**Reads as slop or fatigue**
- Borrowed famous styles such as Ghibli (backlash, above).
- Plastic, glossy, over-smoothed people.
- Generic purple-nebula "mystical" art (inference).
- Toy-box and starter-pack AI dolls (#StarterPackNoAI, above).
- Characters that drift from post to post. Inconsistency "weakens brand recognition, especially for a mascot"
  (https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/consistent-ai-generated-characters).
- One agency claim says 73% of consumers can spot AI content and handmade elements earn 67% more engagement. I
  could not trace either number to a primary study (unverified)
  (https://designmagazine.com.au/anti-ai-crafting-the-50-million-handmade-rebellion-reshaping-design-in-2026/).

**How brands keep a consistent mascot or character system**
1. **One canonical reference per character, locked first.** Then a "mascot bible": turnarounds, expressions,
   poses and outfits (https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/consistent-ai-generated-characters).
2. **Tool workflow (as of 2026, vendor claims):**
   - Midjourney Omni Reference (`--oref`, weight `--ow`) for stylised variations.
   - Google's Nano Banana 2 (Feb 2026) for multi-character, multi-scene consistency: up to 14 reference images per
     call.
   - A common hybrid: Midjourney base, then Nano Banana scenes.
   (https://martini.art/en/blog/nano-banana-2-workflows,
   https://ciguleva.substack.com/p/step-by-step-guide-consistent-characters,
   https://www.lovart.ai/de/blog/midjourney-character-reference-vs-lovart-nano-banana-consistency-test)
3. **A design that survives simplification.** Labubu's appeal is a design that is "instantly recognizable and
   highly adaptable", easy to dress for any context (Labubu explainer in search results; source not pinned, possibly https://lifestyleasia-onemega.com/?p=113158).
   Duolingo's Duo works as a persona with a voice, not just a drawing (Drum, above).
4. **Mizaj-specific advice (inference):**
   - Make the mascots non-human or semi-abstract (elemental creatures or emblem characters) rather than realistic
     people. This avoids uncanny AI faces, sidesteps the "too local" problem of national dress, keeps every
     culture in the audience included, and makes the characters easy to redraw by hand or AI.
   - Add texture last, in code: grain, a 2-colour overprint and a paper texture applied in
     `tools/render_social.py`, so every image looks like one family whatever tool generated it.

## Recommendations for Mizaj

Context:
- `content/STYLE.md` v2 (28 Oct 2026) repositions Mizaj as "international first, desi as the seasoning".
- Current art (`docs/handoff/ART_BRIEF.md`) is flat editorial illustration of people in Pakistani scenes, and
  current carousels (`social/media/`) use local situations such as shaadi, dholki and load-shedding.
- Below are three distinct directions. Each meets the hard rules: no 16-type framework names, no real people, no
  medical claims, astrology and palm reading "for fun", no dating framing.

### Direction A: "Mizaj Deck" (collectible character cards)

- **Mood:** playful-premium and collectible, like trading cards and blind-box culture. Something you'd want to
  collect, trade and compare.
- **Palette:**
  - Cloud-white card stock `#F4F1EA` with ink `#17131F`.
  - Four element colours used as card "suits":
    - Air: rose coral `#E07A6B`
    - Fire: saffron `#E8A33D`
    - Water: deep teal `#1F7A7A`
    - Earth: plum-umber `#5A4A78`
  - A metallic accent (gold foil look) only on rare combinations.
  - Keep the four family colours as small corner pips.
- **Type:** a chunky, characterful serif for card names (Google Fonts: Fraunces or Young Serif), a mono for stats
  (Space Mono or JetBrains Mono), and Inter for body text.
- **Illustration:**
  - 16 type characters as **small, non-realistic figures**: rounded, gender-neutral "spirits" with one prop each
    (the Poet with a notebook, the Host with a teapot).
  - Each character can appear in 4 element variants (colour, aura and texture change; the body stays the same),
    giving 64 collectible cards.
  - A human illustrator draws the master sheet. AI extends it with the locked-reference workflow (section 6).
    Add grain and overprint in the renderer.
